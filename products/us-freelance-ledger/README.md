# Freelance Ledger US

US-only, English-only, USD-only local recordkeeping for self-employed users.

- Cash or accrual method selection; locked after the first transaction.
- Income/expense records, payer/payee, supporting-document reference and memo.
- Schedule C preparation categories and annual category summary.
- CSV export, SQLite backup and audit log.
- No cloud account, bank credentials, tax return filing or tax deduction decisions.

IRS guidance permits a recordkeeping system suited to the business that clearly shows income and expenses and explains cash/accrual methods. This app records user-entered data; it does not determine deductibility or file Schedule C.

Store availability: **United States (US) only**.
