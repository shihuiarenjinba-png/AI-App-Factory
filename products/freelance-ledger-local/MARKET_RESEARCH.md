# Market research — Freelance Ledger Local

Date: 2026-10-31

## Problem

Freelancers need an understandable transaction record that they can retain and hand to an accountant or tax preparer. The product intentionally does not compete on cloud filing, bank feeds or payment processing. It competes on **local ownership, no account, no subscription, simple country-specific bookkeeping and exportability**.

## Official recordkeeping basis

Japan:
- National Tax Agency guidance describes bookkeeping and retention requirements for individual business operators and blue-return bookkeeping. It lists journals/ledgers and explains that records are generally retained for years depending on the record type.
- https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/01.htm
- https://www.keisan.nta.go.jp/r3yokuaru/aoiroshinkoku/aoiroshinkokuseido/aoiroshinkokuseido/seido1.html
- https://www.keisan.nta.go.jp/r5yokuaru/shohizei/ippankazei/shinkokunozei/chobokisaihozon.html

United States:
- IRS recordkeeping guidance says a business may use any system suited to it that clearly shows income and expenses, and describes journals and ledgers as ordinary ways to summarize business transactions.
- https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/recordkeeping
- https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-should-i-record-my-business-transactions
- https://www.irs.gov/publications/p583

## Current competitors

Japan:
- freee Accounting: cloud bookkeeping/tax-return plans; official pricing shows recurring paid plans.
  https://www.freee.co.jp/accounting/individual/pricing/
- Money Forward Cloud Tax Return: cloud tax filing/bookkeeping with recurring plans.
  https://biz.moneyforward.com/tax_return/price
- Yayoi: both online and installed products; installed blue-return product is a strong local competitor.
  https://www.yayoi-kk.co.jp/shinkoku/aoiroshinkoku/yayoiaoiro/price/

US:
- QuickBooks Solopreneur: cloud/mobile, receipt capture, categorization, tax-related workflows.
  https://quickbooks.intuit.com/solopreneur/mobile-app/
- FreshBooks: subscription invoicing/expense/accounting plans.
  https://www.freshbooks.com/pricing
- GnuCash: free open-source desktop accounting, a strong free alternative.
  https://www.gnucash.org/

## Why-pay hypothesis

A one-time Windows utility can be attractive to a user who does **not** want bank credentials, cloud sync, an account or a recurring subscription, but wants a cleaner freelancer-focused workflow than a general double-entry package.

This hypothesis is not yet validated by customer interviews or Microsoft Store conversion data, so the product remains MARKET_FIT_HOLD until direct evidence exists.
