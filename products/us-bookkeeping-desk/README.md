# Double-Entry Bookkeeping US

US-only, English-only, USD-only local double-entry bookkeeping.

- Cash or accrual method selection before the first journal entry.
- Journal, general-ledger core, trial balance, P&L and balance sheet.
- Custom chart of accounts, period lock and CSV export.
- No cloud account, bank feed, payroll, tax filing or automatic deduction decisions.

IRS Publication 583 describes cash and accrual methods and explains that double-entry bookkeeping uses journals and ledgers with built-in checks and balances. This app supports bookkeeping records but does not determine federal or state tax treatment.

Store availability: **United States only**.
